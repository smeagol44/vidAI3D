# VIDA 3D

VIDA 3D is a 3D aquatic artificial-life simulator featuring hinged voxel organisms, genetic controllers and a conserved chemical-energy economy.

**Play:** https://smeagol44.github.io/vidAI3D/

This Wiki describes the **currently implemented features**, not a development history. Only pages actively maintained during feature work are linked:

**Limitations:** Useful locomotion, successful independent foraging and long-term ecosystem survival are still under investigation.
